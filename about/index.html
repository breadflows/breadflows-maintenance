BreadFlows brings original music, videos and selected creative partners together in one place.

Browsing & playback

You can watch and listen without an account. This version has no sign-in, uploads or on-site comments. Preview positions are remembered only while the page is open.

External players & purchases

Muted previews connect to YouTube on supported pointer devices or when you choose Preview. Automatic previews respect reduced-motion settings. Full videos connect when you play them. The AIU.FM player connects automatically on Browse and the radio page. Spotify players connect when you choose Open Spotify player. Those services may process device or playback information under their own policies. Shop checkout links open with the seller, who handles payment, shipping and returns.

Contact & privacy requests

The enquiry form opens your email app with a draft; it does not submit or store your details on this website. Send enquiries or privacy requests to contact@breadflows.com.

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when serving the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
